Every number says where it came from

Analysts already read one convention fluently: blue means a human typed it, black means the model produced it. Fondok keeps that and adds what Excel can't — a mark for document-sourced values, a mark for cross-module links, and a corner flag for overrides and low confidence. Two colours in the default view. Everything else is a 6px geometric mark that disappears until you look for it.

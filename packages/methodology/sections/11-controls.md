::: conceptual {title="What is yours to set, and what the model derives"}
::: intro
A short summary of the division of labour.
:::

::: card
::: columns
::: column {icon=Pencil}
#### Analyst-controlled

- Deal details: name, market, submarket, keys, brands, operating model, positioning, dates.
- Purchase price and the acquisition close date.
- Every financing term, covenant threshold and refinancing assumption.
- Return targets and the return profile.
- Renovation displacement, the stabilization year, the hold period and the exit NOI basis.
- Any assumption override, each with a justification note.
- The IC recommendation and the diligence status of each flag.

:::

::: column {icon=BarChart3}
#### Model-derived

- Every extracted figure, with its page and confidence.
- Year-1 anchors from the primary statement and the growth of later years.
- Sources & Uses, the debt schedule, coverage ratios and the cash flow statement.
- Returns, the sale value, the sensitivity grids and the partnership waterfall.
- Variance flags and the model's own assessment of the Base Case.
- The source and lineage of every number on screen.

:::

:::

{testid=methodology-hold-defaults} **Hold period defaults.** Until you set the hold, the model applies a labelled default by deal type:
5 years for an acquisition, 7 years for a ground-up development and 6 years for an adaptive reuse. A
development or adaptive-reuse hold is badged “Default for development deals — confirm” wherever it
is printed, with the reason, so a default is never mistaken for a figure from the deal. Your hold
period override replaces it.

:::

:::
